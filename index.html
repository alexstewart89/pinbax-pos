Why Shopify POS, not Square plus a custom till

The operational problems we talked through on 9 October, and how a native Shopify POS setup deals with each one.

Today

Stock lives in several places: the Visualsoft site, Square at Cowes, Square and Visualsoft at HQ. That's produced stock mismatches and missed orders.

With Shopify POS

Every sale, online or in either store, deducts from one Shopify stock position immediately. Your new back-office system connects to Shopify admin once, instead of three or four systems trying to talk to each other.

Today

Building and maintaining a custom till means your developers own the payment flow, the hardware connection and every edge case, on top of the warehouse system they're already building.

With Shopify POS

Shopify maintains the POS app, the card readers and the payment integration. Shopify card readers only work through the Shopify POS app, so there's no custom connector to build or keep alive. Your team's time goes into the warehouse system where it adds value.

Today

A customer who buys in Cowes in August and online in November is two separate people in two separate systems. Store customers rarely make it onto the email list.

With Shopify POS

One customer profile across web and stores. Staff send email receipts in a tap, and Shop Pay recognises returning customers from the card they pay with, no form-filling at the till. Summer Cowes customers become winter online customers.

Today

Click and collect, showroom purchases and shipping bulky kit from store are handled around the edges of separate systems.

With Shopify POS

Buy online, pick up at HQ. Buy in store, ship to the customer's home. Returns and exchanges at either location regardless of where the order was placed. One gift card that works online and in both stores. All native, no apps or workarounds.

Today

Boats are high value (£8k to £40k), mostly quote-led and paid by bank transfer, and standard boats like Toppers sit alongside configure-to-order models like the 505.

With Shopify POS

List every boat as a product, then choose per channel: sellable online, sellable in store only, or display with a quote request. Draft orders handle quotes and invoices, and the sale is still recorded against stock and the customer when it lands.

Today

Store staff can't see what's happening online, and head office can't see store performance without pulling it from Square separately.

With Shopify POS

Store staff see online abandoned checkouts and customer history. Head office gets retail reporting by location, product and staff member in the same admin as the website. Unlimited staff logins with role-based permissions on POS Pro.

Suggested sequence

Timed so the offer's deadlines work in your favour rather than against you, given a start-of-2027 go-live and a quieter winter in Cowes.

  1. Now: Advanced plan set up with Elisa, Visualsoft build continues, back-office system connects to Shopify admin.
  2. When the stores are ready to trade on Shopify: add POS Pro yearly at Cowes and HQ. The 7-day hardware window and the 90-day setup and rebate windows all start here, so don't switch it on months early.
  3. Within 7 days: redeem the hardware code for two Victa Mobile readers.
  4. Within 90 days: book the setup call, migrate, go live, and trade through Shopify Payments. $10k USD of in-person sales in that window triggers the £155 rebate.
  5. Later: add a third location at the same per-location price when needed.

Eligibility and the small print

Who qualifies: retailers new to POS Pro. P&B qualifies as a new Shopify store that hasn't used POS Pro before. No revenue minimum.

Hardware: one Verifone Victa Mobile per POS Pro location, £359 list value, for a $10 activation fee (or local equivalent) each. Code arrives by email and must be used within 7 days of adding the first Pro location. Yours to keep.

Rebate: £155 credit against POS subscription fees once $10,000 USD has been processed through Shopify Payments within 90 days of subscribing. Shows in your account in 2 to 5 business days.

Term: 12 months, billed monthly at £52 per location. Early cancellation means paying the remaining months. Auto-renews at the same discount with a reminder beforehand. Upgrading to Plus releases you from the remaining term.
